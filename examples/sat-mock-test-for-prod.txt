[verbal module 1]

1. The researcher checked the measurements three times to ensure their _____. Which choice completes the text most precisely?
a. accuracy
b. distance
c. popularity
d. silence
Answer: A
Explanation: Repeated checking helps ensure that measurements are accurate.

2. The museum opened a new exhibit on Monday. By Friday, more than 500 people had visited it. Which statement is supported by the text?
a. The museum was closed on Friday.
b. More than 500 people visited the new exhibit by Friday.
c. The exhibit opened on Friday.
d. Exactly 500 people visited on Monday.
Answer: B
Explanation: The passage explicitly states that more than 500 people had visited by Friday.

[verbal module 2]

1. Each of the students _____ a notebook. Which choice completes the sentence using Standard English?
a. have
b. are having
c. has
d. were having
Answer: C
Explanation: The singular subject Each takes the singular verb has.

2. The first design was inexpensive to produce. _____, it broke easily during testing. Which transition best completes the text?
a. For example
b. Similarly
c. Therefore
d. However
Answer: D
Explanation: However introduces a contrast between low cost and poor durability.

[math module 1]

1. If 3x + 2 = 17, what is the value of x?
a. 3
b. 5
c. 7
d. 15
Answer: B
Explanation: Subtract 2 to get 3x = 15, then divide by 3 to get x = 5.

2. A rectangle has length 8 and width 3. What is its area?
a. 11
b. 22
c. 24
d. 64
Answer: C
Explanation: Area equals length times width: 8 times 3 = 24.

[math module 2]

1. If f(x) = x^2 + 1, what is f(4)?
a. 17
b. 9
c. 16
d. 5
Answer: A
Explanation: f(4) = 4^2 + 1 = 17.

2. A bag contains 3 red balls and 2 blue balls. One ball is selected at random. What is the probability that it is blue?
a. 1/5
b. 3/5
c. 1/2
d. 2/5
Answer: D
Explanation: There are 2 blue balls out of 5 equally likely balls.
